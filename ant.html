Ants!

Ants are fascinating creatures that are found all over the world. They are known for their ability to work together in colonies and perform complex tasks.

Ants are important!

Ants are important for the environment because they help to break down dead plants and animals, which helps to keep the soil healthy.

Some of my favorite Ants!

Weaver Ant

Weaver ants are a type of ant that are known for their ability to create nests using leaves and twigs.

Leaf Cutter Ant

Leaf cutter ants are a type of ant that are known for their ability to cut and transport leaves.

Bullet Ant

Bullet ants are a type of ant that are known for their powerful sting.
