Bugs are important!

Without bugs, we wouldn't have pollination, which is how plants reproduce. We also wouldn't have the food chain, which is how energy is transferred from one organism to another. Bugs are also important for the environment because they help to break down dead plants and animals, which helps to keep the soil healthy.
