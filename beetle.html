Beetles!

Beetles are fascinating insects that are found all over the world. They are known for their hard shells and diverse forms.

Beetles are important!

Beetles are important for the environment because they help to break down dead plants and animals, which helps to keep the soil healthy.

Some of my favorite Beetles!

Giraffe Weevils

Giraffe weevils are a type of beetle that are known for their distinctive appearance and unique behavior.

Stag Beetles

Stag beetles are a type of beetle that are known for their large mandibles and impressive size.

Lady Bug

Lady bugs are a type of beetle that are known for their bright colors and beneficial role in controlling aphid populations.
